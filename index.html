How it works

From blank page to
presenting in four steps.

1

Answer a few questions

Topic, slide count (1–10, or let Lektura decide), audience, level of detail, and anything to include or skip.

2

Lektura builds the deck

It researches facts and sources, writes the content, designs the layout and checks quality — all before you see a slide.

3

Make it yours

Edit text, add, remove or rearrange slides, move elements around, or switch the theme.

4

Export and present

Download a PowerPoint file, generate a narrated video, present in the app, or share for feedback.
